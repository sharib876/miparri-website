Our story

Built from a belief in belonging.

MiParri was born from a simple question: What if students had a companion that made it easier to find their people, access meaningful support, and navigate everyday student life?

The name draws on "parri," a Jamaican word for friends, and on the friendships that made the founder's own university journey possible.

Founded by Shari-Ann Baker, an education and program management professional with more than a decade of experience, MiParri brings together a passion for student success, connection, and access to opportunity.

The goal is to create something students genuinely want to use, and something that helps them feel seen, supported, and capable of moving forward.
